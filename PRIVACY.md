# Privacy Policy — Gardener's Gambit

**Last updated: June 28, 2026**

## 1. Overview
Gardener's Gambit ("the Game") is a Reddit app built on the Devvit platform. We are committed to minimal data collection.

## 2. Data We Collect
| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Reddit username | Display name on published levels; daily puzzle attribution |
| Daily puzzle results (win/loss, move count) | Leaderboard and daily score tracking |
| User-created map data (grid layout, title) | Publishing and displaying custom levels |
| Skin/preference selections | Saving your in-game customisations |

We do **not** collect: email addresses, passwords, payment information, device identifiers, or location data.

## 3. How Data Is Stored
All data is stored within Reddit's Devvit infrastructure (Redis key-value store). We do not operate external databases or third-party analytics.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Published level data (grid, title, username) is visible to all Reddit users by nature of being a public post.

## 5. Data Retention
Data persists as long as the Game is installed on r/GardenersGambit. If the Game is removed, Reddit/Devvit data retention policies apply.

## 6. Children
The Game does not knowingly collect data from users under 13. Reddit's own age requirements apply.

## 7. Your Rights
To request deletion of your data (e.g. a published level), contact us via modmail at r/GardenersGambit or email aneeshakc88@gmail.com.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted to r/GardenersGambit.
